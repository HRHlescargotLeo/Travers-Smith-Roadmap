Six ideas for traverssmith.com

Clickable prototypes of six improvements to the Travers Smith website, from service and profile pages to events, sign-up and the international offer. They are greyscale on purpose: this round is about what goes on each page and how it behaves, not how it looks.

Version 1.1 29 September 2026 Greyscale prototypes Sample content marked

The prototypes

  1. Service page
    1

    A service page that proves it

    Corporate M&A reordered around the evidence: what the team does, dated statistics, a case study, a feed of deals only, directory quotes with their year, four key contacts and a short form for sending the practice a brief.

    Try: filter the deals to Public M&A, then send the team a brief.

    Open prototype
  2. Partner profile
    2

    Profiles that show the work

    Shown with Emma Havas, and working for every lawyer named in the prototypes: an at-a-glance panel, selected deals that can be filtered and link to their press releases, and a feed of her deals, briefings, podcasts and events.

    Try: open Experience and show only sales and exits, then click any lawyer named elsewhere in the prototypes.

    Open prototype
  3. Briefing and topic hub
    3

    A briefing page that keeps readers

    Key points at the top, reading time, chapter links you can share, and somewhere to go next. With it, a topic hub for pensions consolidation: timeline, every briefing and event, and who to call.

    Try: copy the link to question 7, then follow Pensions.

    Open prototype
  4. Events
    4

    Events people can plan around

    Upcoming, on-demand and past events kept apart, full dates and time zones, add to calendar, and a short registration form on the event page itself.

    Try: register for the Ukraine webinar, then open a past event such as Future of Fintech 2026.

    Open prototype
  5. Sign-up and contact
    5

    Stay in touch on the reader's terms

    A sign-up that asks for topics before details, and a contact page that asks the reason first, routes it and says what happens next.

    Try: on Contact, choose "Media enquiry", then "A new legal matter".

    Open prototype
  6. International
    6

    The international network, made visible

    The relationship-firm model in three steps, one set of figures, and a page per region with jurisdictions, recent matters and who to call.

    Try: open Europe, then switch to Asia Pacific.

    Open prototype

How to review

  • Everything interactive works: filters, tabs, following topics, add to calendar, region switching and every form, including validation. Links to pages outside these prototypes go nowhere by design.
  • Use the bar at the top to move between prototypes. Switch Notes on (top right) to see what each prototype proposes and why, plus notes on behaviour (yellow) and open questions for Travers Smith (red). Notes start switched off.
  • People (with shortened profiles), deals, briefings and past events are from traverssmith.com. Case studies, some upcoming events and the regional detail are samples, marked as such.
  • Site search, navigation and the Client Area are covered by separate projects and are deliberately left out.

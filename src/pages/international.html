What we're proposing

The International page explains the model in three steps, then lets the reader pick a region. Each regional page lists the jurisdictions the firm works in regularly, examples of recent matters and who to call. The headline figures come from one source, so the copy and the statistics can't disagree.

Why

The firm works with independent firms rather than opening offices, which is a real point of difference but a hard one to picture. The live page has two case studies and two statistics, says "over 150 firms" in the copy next to "160+" in the numbers, and its news feed's newest item is from January. Clients asking "do you cover Brazil?" have to phone to find out.

The model, statistics, case studies and International Board are from traverssmith.com. Regional figures, jurisdictions and most matters are samples for discussion. Ideas 30–32.

How it works

  1. We choose the firm

    From over a decade of working relationships, we pick independent firms and individual lawyers with the right issue and sector experience in each jurisdiction, and we can recommend local firms impartially.

  2. One engagement, one fee arrangement

    We regularly coordinate cross-border advice through a single engagement letter and fee arrangement, so you deal with one team.

  3. We take responsibility

    In appropriate cases we take responsibility for the work of the firms we partner with, and we manage conflicts and price in every case.

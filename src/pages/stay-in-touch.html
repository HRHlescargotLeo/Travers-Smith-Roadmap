What we're proposing

Sign-up starts with what the reader wants to hear about: the firm's named series, practice areas and events. Only then does it ask for a name and email, with organisation and job title optional and no "How did you hear about us?". Readers choose between getting things as they are published or a weekly digest.

Why

The live form asks for six required fields, including how you heard about the firm, and only offers topics "once you have submitted your details". Someone who wants the pensions newsletter has to hand over their job title before they can say so. Topics followed from a briefing or service page arrive here already ticked.

Series names are from the firm's Knowledge pages. Ideas 25, 26 and 29.

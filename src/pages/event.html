What we're proposing

The essentials in one panel at the top: date, time and time zone, duration, format, where, CPD and an add-to-calendar option. Registration is a short form on the page itself, with a confirmation that says what happens next and offers the calendar file straight away.

Why

The live webinar page gives "03:00 PM UK time" and nothing about duration or platform. Registration sits in an 800px frame from the marketing platform, with a square navy button unlike the rest of the site and a stretch of empty space below it.

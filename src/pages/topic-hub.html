Pensions consolidation

The Government plans to move millions of small, dormant pension pots into authorised consolidator schemes automatically. Here is what is proposed, when it happens and what DC schemes, providers and employers should do now.

What happens when

The framework

The Pension Schemes Act 2026 sets the framework for consolidating small pots. The detail follows in regulations.

The Government's latest consultation

Covers which schemes and pots are in scope, possible exclusions (guarantees, protected pension ages, Sharia-compliant funds), what members are told, authorisation of consolidators and new employer duties.

Read our 10 key answers

Draft regulations, TPR and FCA

A consultation on draft regulations, with further detail through TPR Codes of Practice and FCA rules consulted on alongside.

The remaining detail

A further consultation on ceding scheme duties, supervisory detail, final data standards and the rest of the framework.

Transfers begin

The Government expects to implement the multiple default consolidator solution from 2030, starting between April and June, subject to a review of the digital infrastructure.

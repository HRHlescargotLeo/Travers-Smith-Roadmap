Prototype 2 of 6 · Partner profile, shown with Emma Havas

What we're proposing

A profile that shows the work as well as the biography. An at-a-glance panel beside the introduction, the selected deals as a list that can be filtered and links through to the press release where there is one, and a feed of the partner's own briefings, podcasts, events and deals, filled automatically from content where they are named.

Why

Emma's current profile lists 17 selected deals with no years and no links, although the most recent (the Logiq sale to IBM) has its own press release. The profile carries no articles or talks, even though briefing and event pages already name people as contacts and speakers, so the connection exists in the CMS and simply isn't shown.

Name, role, practices, recognition, deals and the client quote are from traverssmith.com. Items marked Sample, and bracketed fields, are placeholders. Ideas 8–10.

Emma is a corporate partner specialising in pan-European and international private equity and M&A transactions.

Her clients include institutional investors, alternative asset managers, corporates, senior management teams, founders and investee companies. She advises on leveraged buyouts, consortium deals, bolt-on acquisitions, carve-outs, co-investments, minority deals, recapitalisations, divestments, distressed M&A, public-to-privates, joint ventures and restructurings.

Emma also advises the firm's global asset management clients on GP-stake transactions, GP succession planning and structured secondary market liquidity, including GP-led and LP-led secondary transactions.

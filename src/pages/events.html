Prototype 4 of 6 · Events listing and event page

What we're proposing

Upcoming events first, with on-demand and past events on their own tabs. Every card gives the full date with the year, the time with its time zone, how long it lasts, the format and an add-to-calendar option. Past events link to a recording or the takeaways where there is one. The event page puts the essentials at the top and replaces the embedded registration frame with a short form in the site's own style.

Why

On 29 September the live Events page mixes 36 past and future results, holds one item under "Future events", and still promotes "ESG Academy 2024". The homepage's "Latest events" shows three past events while the 14 October Ukraine defence webinar goes unmentioned. Anyone planning their diary has to work out which events are still to come.

The Ukraine webinar and past events are real; other upcoming events are samples. Ideas 19–23.

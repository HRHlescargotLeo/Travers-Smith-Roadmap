Prototype 6 of 6 · Regional page

What we're proposing

One page per region answering the questions a client actually asks: do you cover this country, what have you done there recently, and who do I call? The same template serves all four regions, and the region can be switched without leaving the page.

Why

The International page currently says the firm works in over 50 countries without saying which ones. A regional page makes the relationship-firm model concrete, and gives BD somewhere to send a client who asks about a specific market.

All regional content is sample content written for discussion, apart from matters without a Sample tag, which come from the firm's press releases. Idea 30.

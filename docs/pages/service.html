What we're proposing

The same service page, reordered so that the evidence comes before the people. A short intro, a list of the kinds of matter the team handles, the statistics with a date and source under each, a case study, a feed that only contains deals, and directory quotes with their year. Then four key contacts, the rest of the team one click away, and a short form for sending the practice a brief.

Why

The current Corporate M&A page has strong material but no case study, and the numbers and quotes carry no date, so a reader can't tell whether "the last 24 months" means this year or three years ago. Twenty partner cards sit between the statistics and the proof. The deal feed also includes a Senior Counsel promotions announcement, which is good news but not a deal.

Case studies are samples written for the prototype. Deals, people and quotes are from traverssmith.com. Ideas 1, 3–7 on the opportunities page.

Corporate M&A

Whether furthering growth or pursuing an investment, M&A is one of the most significant and high-pressured processes a company goes through. We act for buyers, sellers, boards and investors on public takeovers and private deals, and over half of our work has an international component.

Case study Sample

Separating a UK-listed industrial group's European division

11jurisdictions
1engagement letter
7practices involved

The challenge

A FTSE 250 group wanted to sell a division with operating companies across Europe, on a timetable set by its half-year results.

What we did

We ran the separation and sale from London, coordinating our relationship firms in each jurisdiction under a single engagement letter and fee arrangement, with Tax, Employment, Pensions and Competition in the core team.

The outcome

Signed and announced alongside the results, with completion three months later.

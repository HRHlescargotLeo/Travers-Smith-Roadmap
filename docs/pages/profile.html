Prototype 2 of 6 · Lawyer profile (opens Emma Havas by default)

What we're proposing

A profile that shows the work as well as the biography. An at-a-glance panel beside a short introduction, the selected deals as a list that can be filtered and links through to the press release where there is one, and a feed of the lawyer's own briefings, events and deals, filled automatically from content where they are named.

Why

Emma Havas's current profile lists 17 selected deals with no years and no links, although the most recent (the Logiq sale to IBM) has its own press release. Profiles carry no articles or talks, even though briefing and event pages already name people as contacts and speakers, so the connection exists in the CMS and simply isn't shown.

Every lawyer named in these prototypes opens here with their own profile, shortened from traverssmith.com. Bracketed fields are placeholders. Ideas 8–10.

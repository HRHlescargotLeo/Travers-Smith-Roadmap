Prototype 5 of 6 · Contact, with the sign-up alongside

What we're proposing

Contact asks what the enquiry is about before anything else. A new legal matter gets a short form routed to the right practice. Media, careers and existing clients are shown who to contact directly, without filling in a form at all. Data protection requests get their own short form. Every route says what happens next.

Why

The live form has nine fields, seven of them required, and a single drop-down with 12 kinds of query, from new work to audio transcript corrections. A journalist on deadline and a GC with a new deal fill in the same form, including job title and company, and nothing says when anyone will reply.

Addresses and switchboard numbers are from the live page. Ideas 27 and 28.
